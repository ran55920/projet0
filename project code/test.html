Medical Guidelines for Children of the Moon 🌙

Medical Catalog 📖

Emergency Handling

In case of sunburn or other emergencies:

  • Seek immediate medical attention.
  • Use cold compresses to soothe burns.
  • Keep emergency contacts handy.

Lighting Precautions

To protect against UV exposure:

  • Avoid direct sunlight exposure.
  • Use UV-blocking window films.
  • Wear protective clothing and hats.

Medications

Follow these guidelines for medications:

  • Follow prescribed schedules strictly.
  • Consult a doctor before starting new treatments.
  • Store medications in a cool, dry place.
